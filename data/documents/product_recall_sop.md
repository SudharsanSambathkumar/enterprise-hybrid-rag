# Product Recall SOP

## Scope
This SOP applies to every product recall raised by a supplier, by the DailyBasket QA team, or by a food safety authority. Store managers own execution in their store.

## Step 1: Pull Stock
Remove all affected stock from shelves, back store and cold rooms within 2 hours of the recall alert. Check batch numbers against the recall notice.

## Step 2: Quarantine
Move recalled stock to the red-tagged quarantine area. Recalled items must never be returned to sale, donated, or discarded before the supplier collects them.

## Step 3: Notify Customers
Notify affected loyalty customers by SMS and app notification within 24 hours. Put the recall notice at the store entrance and at the customer service desk.

## Step 4: Log and Report
Log the recall in the QA portal with batch numbers, quantity pulled and photos. The regional operations manager must be informed by phone for any recall involving dairy, baby food or meat.

## Step 5: Refunds
Customers returning recalled products get a full refund without a bill. Refunds are booked against the supplier's credit note.
