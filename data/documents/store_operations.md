# Store Operations Manual

## Opening Checklist
The store manager or shift lead opens the store 45 minutes before trading hours: disarm the alarm, check cold chain logs, count the opening cash float, and confirm all billing counters are online.

## Cash Handling
Each billing counter starts with a cash float of Rs. 3,000. Cash above Rs. 20,000 in a counter must be moved to the safe. The day-end cash report is uploaded before 11 PM.

## Escalation Matrix
Store issues are escalated to the store manager first, then the regional operations manager. Safety incidents and recalls are escalated to the regional manager immediately by phone.

## Stock Audits
A cycle count of the top 200 SKUs is done every Monday. Variance above 2 percent must be explained in the audit portal within 48 hours.
