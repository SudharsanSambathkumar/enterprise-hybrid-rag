# Pricing and Promotions Policy

## Price Changes
Price changes are pushed centrally every night at 2 AM. Store staff must not edit prices at the billing counter.

## Shelf Labels
Shelf labels must be updated before the store opens. If the shelf price and billing price differ, the customer pays the lower price.

## Markdowns
Bakery items are marked down by 30 percent after 8 PM on their last sale day. Dairy markdowns need store manager approval.

## Loyalty Points
Customers earn 1 loyalty point for every Rs. 100 spent. Points expire after 12 months.
