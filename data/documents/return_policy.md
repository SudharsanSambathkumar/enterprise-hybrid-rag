# Customer Return Policy

## Non-Perishable Items
Non-perishable items can be returned within 30 days with the original bill, if the packaging is unopened.

## Perishable Items
Fresh produce, dairy and bakery items can only be returned within 24 hours of purchase, and only if they are spoiled or past their expiry date.

## Electronics and Appliances
Small appliances can be exchanged within 7 days for a manufacturing defect. After 7 days, customers are referred to the brand's service centre.

## Refund Mode
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Cash refunds above Rs. 5,000 require store manager approval.
