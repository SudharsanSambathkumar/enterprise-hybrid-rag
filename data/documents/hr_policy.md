# HR Policy

## Sick Leave
Store staff are entitled to 12 paid sick leaves per calendar year.
Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate submitted to the store manager within 3 working days of returning.
Unused sick leave does not carry forward to the next year and is not encashable.

## Casual Leave
All employees get 8 casual leaves per year. Casual leave must be applied for at least 1 day in advance through the HR portal, except in emergencies.
No more than 3 casual leaves can be taken in a row.

## Earned Leave
Employees earn 15 days of earned leave per year after completing 6 months of service.
Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward. Earned leave during the festive freeze (15 Oct to 15 Nov) needs regional manager approval.

## Shift Swaps
Staff may swap shifts with a colleague of the same role. Both employees must log the swap in the rostering app at least 12 hours before the shift starts. The store manager approves swaps.

## Attendance
Staff must clock in using the biometric terminal. Arriving more than 15 minutes late three times in a month results in a written warning.
