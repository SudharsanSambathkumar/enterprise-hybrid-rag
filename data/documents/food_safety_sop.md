# Food Safety SOP

## Cold Chain
Dairy products must be stored between 0 and 4 degrees Celsius. Frozen products must be stored at minus 18 degrees Celsius or below.
Cold room and chiller temperatures are logged every 4 hours in the temperature register.

## Temperature Breach
If a chiller is above 5 degrees Celsius for more than 30 minutes, move the stock to a working chiller and inform the store manager. Stock that stayed above 8 degrees Celsius for over 2 hours must be quarantined.

## Expiry Management
Follow FEFO (First Expiry, First Out). Products are removed from shelves 1 day before their expiry date for dairy, and 3 days before for packaged foods.

## Hygiene
Staff handling open food must wear gloves and hairnets. Hand-wash stations are checked every shift.
